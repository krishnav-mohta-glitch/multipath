## 2. Authoritative Board Subject Structure & Statutory Classification

### A. ICSE Subject & Examination Code Structure (Classes 9–10)
In accordance with official CISCE examination regulations (*ICSE Regulations and Syllabuses for 2027 and 2028 Examinations*, cisce.org):

1. **History, Civics and Geography (Official Subject Code: 50)**:
   - **Classification**: Counted as **ONE official examination subject under Code 50**, NOT two separate subjects.
   - **Component Papers**: Examined through two compulsory written papers:
     - *Paper 1: History & Civics* (2 hours, 80 marks)
     - *Paper 2: Geography* (2 hours, 80 marks)
   - Both papers contribute equally to the candidate's composite grade in Subject Code 50.
2. **Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) (Official Subject Code: 52)**:
   - **Classification**: Counted as **ONE official examination subject under Code 52**, NOT three separately coded examination subjects.
   - **Component Papers**: Comprises three compulsory component papers:
     - *Paper 1: Physics* (2 hours, 80 marks)
     - *Paper 2: Chemistry* (2 hours, 80 marks)
     - *Paper 3: Biology* (2 hours, 80 marks)
   - The three disciplines are syllabus components within Science Code 52. Artificial independent subject codes (such as 53 or 54) have been completely expunged from the application models, mappings, and documentation.
3. **Statutory Grouping of Subjects (Classes 9–10)**:
   - **Group I (Compulsory Core — All candidates must take)**: English (Code 01), Second Language (Code 05), History, Civics and Geography (Code 50).
   - **Group II (Academic Electives — Candidates select 2 or 3 subjects)**: Mathematics (Code 51), Science (Code 52), Commercial Studies (Code 63), Economics (Code 64), Environmental Science (Code 65).
   - **Group III (Applied Electives — Candidates select 1 subject)**: Computer Applications (Code 86), etc.

### B. CISCE Upper Primary Curriculum Framework (Classes 6–8)
In accordance with the official *CISCE Curriculum for Upper Primary Classes (Classes VI–VIII)* (2016 Edition):
- **English**: Compulsory first language (Language and Literature components).
- **Second Language**: Hindi or regional language.
- **Mathematics**: Compulsory core syllabus.
- **Science**: Structured by CISCE into three distinct disciplinary syllabi starting in Class 6 (Physics pp. 42–53, Chemistry pp. 54–65, Biology pp. 66–78), taught as components of Upper Primary Science.
- **History, Civics and Geography**: Structured into two distinct syllabi (History & Civics pp. 80–102, Geography pp. 104–128).
- **Computer Studies**: An explicit curricular area mandated by the Upper Primary framework (pp. 130–148).
- **Third Language**: Mandated under Three-Language provision.

### C. CBSE Secondary Stage (Classes 9–10)
In accordance with the *CBSE Secondary School Curriculum 2026–27* (Vol. 1 Main Subjects & Vol. 2 Skill Subjects):
- **Compulsory Main Subjects**:
  - Mathematics Standard (Code 041) / Mathematics Basic (Code 241)
  - Science (Code 086) — integrated physics, chemistry, biology
  - Social Science (Code 087) — integrated history, geography, civics, economics
  - English Language & Literature (Code 184)
  - Hindi Course A (Code 002) / Course B (Code 085)
- **Academic Electives (Group A2)**:
  - Computer Applications (Code 165)
- **Skill Subjects (Group S / Vocational 6th Subject)**:
  - Information Technology (Code 402)
  - Artificial Intelligence (Code 417)

### D. CBSE Middle Stage (Classes 6–8)
In accordance with the *National Curriculum Framework for School Education (NCF-SE) 2023* (Section 3.2, pp. 64–90) and CBSE Circular Acad-17/2026:
- CBSE middle school does NOT administer board examinations. Board numerical codes do not exist for Classes 6–8.
- Curricular areas are designated under official NCF-SE terminology:
  - Mathematics (`NCF-MATH`) — Prescribed NCERT textbooks: Class 6 *Ganita Prakash*, Class 7 & 8 *Mathematics*.
  - Science (`NCF-SCI`) — Prescribed NCERT textbooks: Class 6 *Curiosity*, Class 7 & 8 *Science*.
  - Social Science (`NCF-SOC`) — Prescribed NCERT textbooks: Class 6 *Exploring Society: India and Beyond*, Class 7 & 8 NCERT social science textbooks.
  - Language 1 (`NCF-LANG-1`) — Prescribed NCERT textbooks: Class 6 *Poorvi*, Class 7 *Honeycomb*, Class 8 *Honeydew*.
  - Language 2 (`NCF-LANG-2`) — Prescribed NCERT textbooks: Class 6 *Malhar*, Class 7 & 8 *Vasant*.
  - Language 3 (`NCF-LANG-3`) — Prescribed NCERT textbooks: Class 6 *Deepakam*, Class 7 & 8 *Ruchira*.
  - Vocational Education (`NCF-VOC`) — CBSE 12-Hour Skill Modules in Coding and Computational Thinking.
