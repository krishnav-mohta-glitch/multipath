## 3. CBSE Class IX Curricular Accounting: 12 Slots vs 13 Offerings

A major point of clarity in the CBSE Secondary Stage 2026–27 curriculum is the relationship between the **12 statutory curricular slots** defined in the Scheme of Studies (Circular Acad-70/2026) and the **13 catalogued board offerings** in MULTIPATH:

### The 12 Statutory Curricular Slots (Circular Acad-70/2026)
1. **Slot 1**: Language 1 (English Language & Literature, Code 184) — Compulsory Language
2. **Slot 2**: Language 2 (Hindi Course A/B, Code 002/085, or regional language) — Compulsory Language
3. **Slot 3**: Optional Language 3 (Sanskrit, regional, or foreign language) — Optional Elective Provision (transitional up to 2028–29 per Circulars Acad-33 & Acad-49)
4. **Slot 4**: Mathematics / Mathematics Advanced (Dual Pathway) — Compulsory Core
5. **Slot 5**: Science / Science Advanced (Dual Pathway) — Compulsory Core
6. **Slot 6**: Social Science (Integrated History, Geography, Political Science, Economics) — Compulsory Core
7. **Slot 7**: **Individuals in Society** — Compulsory Curricular Area (Mandated under Circular Acad-70/2026 & NCF-SE Sec. 4.2)
8. **Slot 8**: **Kaushal Vikas (Vocational Education, Code 444)** — Mandatory Vocational Component
9. **Slot 9**: **Computational Thinking & Artificial Intelligence (CT & AI)** — Compulsory 15-Hour Skill Modules
10. **Slot 10**: Art Education (Visual & Performing Arts) — Compulsory Co-Scholastic Internal Assessment
11. **Slot 11**: Physical Education & Well-being — Compulsory Co-Scholastic Internal Assessment
12. **Slot 12**: Optional Subject from Prescribed Board List — Optional Elective Choice

### Why MULTIPATH Catalogues 13 Offerings for 12 Slots
Under **Slot 12**, CBSE authorizes students to elect either an approved Skill Subject or an Academic Elective. In MULTIPATH, we catalogue the two primary options chosen by secondary students:
- **Option 12A**: Information Technology (`off-cbse-9-it`, Official Code **402**, `skill_elective`)
- **Option 12B**: Computer Applications (`off-cbse-9-ca`, Official Code **165**, `academic_elective`)

Because both IT 402 and CA 165 represent distinct, officially authorized course syllabi with independent educational pathways, MULTIPATH catalogues both entries to provide full learning coverage. **Therefore, Class IX has exactly 12 statutory curricular slots and 13 catalogued board offerings.**

## 4. CBSE Class X Co-Scholastic Deduplication: Work Experience Subsumed

### Statutory Finding & Legal Basis
In earlier draft curricula, *Work Experience* was historically assigned Code 500. However, under the current official CBSE Secondary School Curriculum (Vol. 1 & Vol. 2) and the official CBSE Skill Education Scheme:
> *"Work Experience is not examined as an independent standalone subject. All work-centric, experiential, and pre-vocational activities are officially subsumed within **Health & Physical Education (Subject Code: 502)**."*

### Deduplication Action Taken
1. **Eliminated**: Redundant artificial entry `off-cbse-10-we` (Work Experience, Code 500).
2. **Updated**: Entry `off-cbse-10-pe` (`Health & Physical Education (including Work Experience)`, Official Code **502**, `co_scholastic_internal`).
3. **Class X Accounting Restored**:
   - 5 Compulsory Examination Subjects (Math 041/241, Science 086, Social Science 087, English 184, Hindi 002/085)
   - 3 Permissible Elective Choices (IT 402, AI 417, CA 165 — none simultaneously compulsory)
   - 2 Internal Assessment Areas (Art Education 503, Health & PE including Work Experience 502)
   - **Total Class X Catalogued Offerings**: **10 Offerings** (exactly equal to **10 statutory slots**).

## 5. CISCE ICSE Selection Rules & Mutual Exclusion Pairs

Under the *ICSE Regulations and Syllabuses for 2027 and 2028 Examinations*:

### Group I: Compulsory Core (All Candidates Sit)
- **English (Subject Code: 01)**: Paper 1 Language, Paper 2 Literature.
- **Second Language (Subject Code: 05)**: Hindi or regional Indian language.
- **History, Civics and Geography (Subject Code: 50)**: ONE examination subject comprising Paper 1 (History & Civics) and Paper 2 (Geography).

### Group II: Academic Electives (Candidates Select 2 or 3 Subjects)
- Mathematics (Code: **51**)
- Science (Code: **52**): ONE examination subject comprising Paper 1 (Physics), Paper 2 (Chemistry), and Paper 3 (Biology). *There are zero independent examination codes 53 or 54.*
- Commercial Studies (Code: **63**)
- Economics (Code: **64**)
- Environmental Science (Code: **65**)

### Group III: Applied Electives (Candidates Select 1 or 2 Subjects)
Candidates select 1 or 2 subjects from Section A or Section B. The following **Statutory Mutual Exclusion Pairs** are strictly enforced by CISCE regulations:
1. **Commercial Studies vs Commercial Applications**:
   - Candidates offering **Commercial Studies (Code 63)** in Group II may NOT offer **Commercial Applications (Code 88)** in Group III.
2. **Economics vs Economic Applications**:
   - Candidates offering **Economics (Code 64)** in Group II may NOT offer **Economic Applications (Code 87)** in Group III.
3. **Environmental Science vs Environmental Applications**:
   - Candidates offering **Environmental Science (Code 65)** in Group II may NOT offer **Environmental Applications (Code 89)** in Group III.

### Compulsory Internal Assessment
- **Socially Useful Productive Work and Community Service (SUPW)**: Mandatory school-evaluated internal requirement for the award of the ICSE pass certificate.
